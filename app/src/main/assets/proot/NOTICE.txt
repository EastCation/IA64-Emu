PRoot runtime
=============

The files under app/src/main/assets/proot/<abi>/ are an unmodified Android
build of PRoot (proot, libexec/loader, libexec/loader32) and libtalloc,
taken from the Termux bootstrap used by Vectras VM.

Upstream sources:
  PRoot:    https://github.com/termux/proot
  libtalloc: https://github.com/termux/termux-packages (libtalloc package)
